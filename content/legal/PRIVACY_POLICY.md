## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**(a) Account Information.** When you register for the Service, we collect your name, email address, phone number, job title, clinic or practice name, business address, and payment information. If you register on behalf of an organization, we may also collect the organization's name, address, tax identification number, and billing contact information.

**(b) Clinical Data.** When you use the Service, you submit Clinical Data for analysis, which may include SOAP notes (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan), invoice line items, appointment records, patient identifiers, fee schedules, procedure codes, and other veterinary practice data. Clinical Data may contain information about animal patients and their owners, including pet owner names, addresses, and contact information.

**(c) Communications.** When you contact us for support, submit feedback, or communicate with us by email, phone, chat, or other means, we collect the content of those communications along with associated metadata such as timestamps and sender information.

**(d) Payment Information.** When you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect payment details including credit card numbers, billing addresses, and transaction history. Payment information is processed and stored by our third-party payment processor and is not stored on our servers in unencrypted form.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**(a) Usage Data.** We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, audit frequency, number of audits performed, search queries, clickstream data, and session duration.

**(b) Device and Browser Information.** We collect information about the device and browser you use to access the Service, including IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution, language preferences, and unique device identifiers.

**(c) Log Data.** Our servers automatically record information when you access the Service, including your IP address, the date and time of your request, the referring URL, the pages you visit, and the actions you take within the Service.

**(d) Cookies and Similar Technologies.** We use cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity, maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze Service usage. See Section 8 (Cookies and Tracking Technologies) for more details.

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Sources

**(a) PIMS Integrations.** If you connect your Practice Information Management System (such as ezyVet or Cornerstone) to the Service, we receive Clinical Data directly from those systems in accordance with the permissions you grant.

**(b) Authentication Providers.** If you sign in using a third-party authentication service (such as Google), we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider.

**(c) Analytics Providers.** We may receive aggregated analytics data from third-party analytics services that help us understand how the Service is used.

## 4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information:

### 4.1 Encryption

**(a) In Transit.** All data transmitted between your systems and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS) 1.2 or higher. API communications require HTTPS.

**(b) At Rest.** Clinical Data and other sensitive information stored in our databases is encrypted using AES-256 encryption. Database backups are also encrypted.

### 4.2 Access Controls

- Role-based access controls restrict employee access to customer data on a need-to-know basis
- Multi-factor authentication is required for all VetGuard staff accessing production systems
- Access logs are maintained and reviewed regularly

### 4.3 Infrastructure Security

- The Service is hosted on managed cloud infrastructure with physical security controls, network firewalls, and intrusion detection systems
- Production systems are isolated from development and testing environments
- Regular vulnerability assessments and security audits are conducted

### 4.4 Incident Response

We maintain an incident response plan for security breaches. In the event of a breach affecting your personal information or Clinical Data, we will notify you and applicable regulatory authorities within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.

### 4.5 Employee Training

All VetGuard employees with access to customer data undergo security awareness training and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

### 4.6 Limitations

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the Service at your own risk.

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Active Accounts

We retain your information for the duration of your active subscription as follows:

**(a) Account Information.** Retained for the life of your account.

**(b) Clinical Data.** Raw SOAP notes and invoice data are retained in encrypted form for the duration of your subscription to support auditing, historical analysis, and debugging. Audit Results are retained for the duration of your subscription.

**(c) Usage Data.** Retained for up to twenty-four (24) months for analytics and Service improvement purposes.

**(d) Log Data.** Server logs are retained for up to twelve (12) months for security and debugging purposes.

**(e) Payment Records.** Transaction records are retained for seven (7) years to comply with tax and accounting requirements.

### 5.2 After Account Cancellation

Upon cancellation or termination of your account:

**(a)** Your Clinical Data, Audit Results, and Account Information will be retained for thirty (30) days to facilitate potential reactivation.

**(b)** After the thirty-day retention period, Clinical Data and Audit Results will be permanently deleted from our active systems.

**(c)** Encrypted backups containing your data may persist for up to ninety (90) days as part of our disaster recovery procedures, after which they will be purged.

**(d)** Payment records and transaction history will be retained for seven (7) years as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

**(e)** Aggregated, de-identified data derived from your use of the Service may be retained indefinitely.

### 5.3 Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your Clinical Data at any time by contacting support@vetguard.io. We will process deletion requests within ten (10) business days and confirm completion by email. Please note that deletion requests do not apply to aggregated or de-identified data, data retained for legal or regulatory compliance, or data contained in encrypted backups during the backup retention period.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### 8.1 Types of Cookies We Use

**(a) Essential Cookies.** Required for the Service to function. These cookies enable core features such as authentication, session management, and security. They cannot be disabled without impairing the Service.

**(b) Performance Cookies.** Collect anonymous data about how you use the Service, including pages visited, time spent, and error messages. We use this data to monitor and improve Service performance.

**(c) Functional Cookies.** Remember your preferences and settings (such as language, timezone, and display options) to personalize your experience.

**(d) Analytics Cookies.** Help us understand how visitors interact with the Service through aggregated, anonymized data. We use Google Analytics and similar tools for this purpose.

### 8.2 Managing Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, set cookie expiration policies, and view cookies currently stored. Please note that blocking essential cookies may impair the functionality of the Service.

**Browser-specific instructions:**

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

### 8.3 Third-Party Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect and analyze usage data. Google Analytics uses cookies to track interactions with the Service. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

## 9. AI-Specific Privacy Provisions

### 9.1 How AI Processes Your Data

When you use the Service, your Clinical Data is processed by AI Components as follows:

**(a)** Clinical Data (SOAP notes and invoice items) is transmitted to our AI processing partner (currently Anthropic) via encrypted API calls.

**(b)** The AI model analyzes the Clinical Data to extract clinical events, cross-reference them against invoice items, and generate confidence-scored Audit Results.

**(c)** The AI model's response is returned to VetGuard, parsed, and stored as Audit Results in your account.

**(d)** Each AI processing request is stateless. The AI model does not retain your Clinical Data between requests and does not learn from or store individual customer data.

### 9.2 AI Model Training

Your Clinical Data is not used to train AI models. Our agreement with our AI processing partner (Anthropic) explicitly prohibits the use of customer data for model training purposes. AI models are pre-trained on publicly available data and are not fine-tuned using customer Clinical Data.

### 9.3 AI Processing Location

AI processing occurs on servers located in the United States. Clinical Data transmitted to AI processing partners does not leave the United States unless otherwise disclosed to you in advance.

### 9.4 AI Accuracy and Limitations

AI-generated Audit Results are advisory and may contain errors, omissions, or inaccuracies. You are responsible for reviewing and verifying all Audit Results before taking action. VetGuard does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of AI-generated output. See our Terms of Service for additional disclaimers regarding AI Components.

### 9.5 Human Review

VetGuard employees may access Clinical Data or Audit Results for the following limited purposes: investigating and resolving customer support requests, debugging technical issues with the Service, and monitoring for fraud or abuse. Access is logged and restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
